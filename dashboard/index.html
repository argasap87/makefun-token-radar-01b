How the radar works

What it ranks. The board ranks the protocols the snapshot records as having no token, by what that token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. It never predicts whether or when a token is issued. “Records no token” is what the snapshot says; a protocol on the board may still have a token its record does not carry.

The universe. The 40 protocols on the house watchlist are the floor. Above the floor, every pre-token protocol that passes the watchlist's own exclusion rule joins them — no exchange, no bridge or staking product of a token that already trades, no wrapped or staked name, no version pool. Below the floor, pre-token protocols join by TVL until the board holds 240 rows. The floor is a floor, never a ceiling.

The value. Implied value = TVL × multiple × growth × maturity. The multiple is the category's comparables multiple, shrunk toward the market median and clamped to [0.15, 0.60], so no category can price a protocol at a wild multiple of its TVL. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to [−60%, +120%], at half weight. Maturity is 0.6 plus up to 0.4 for up to three years listed with no token. Every figure comes from the sealed snapshot; no protocol name is ever special-cased.

The farm plan. Each row carries seven steps with what they cost and how long they take, derived from the protocol's own category, chains, TVL and age. Steps are written for the protocol, not copied from a list.

Separation. Protocols whose token already trades are kept apart in the Live view at their market value. The board never mixes a market value into a ranked row.
